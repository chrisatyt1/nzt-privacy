NZT Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

NZT is a personal productivity app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is built for and used by a single person, its developer. It is not offered to the public.

WHOOP data NZT reads

When you connect WHOOP, NZT requests read-only access to your recovery, sleep, and workout records (scopes read:recovery, read:sleep, read:workout, and offline to stay connected). NZT uses completed workouts to mark workout habits as done on the matching day and shows recovery and sleep alongside your daily plan. NZT never writes to your WHOOP account.

Where data is stored

WHOOP data is stored only on your own iPhone and Apple Watch. Your WHOOP access and refresh tokens are kept in the device Keychain. NZT has no account system, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and does not keep a cloud copy of your WHOOP data.

The sign-in service

WHOOP requires a private client secret to complete sign-in. NZT uses a small sign-in service, hosted by the developer, that holds that secret. It exchanges the one-time WHOOP sign-in code for access tokens and renews them when they expire. It returns the tokens directly to the app and does not store, log, or inspect them or any WHOOP health data.

Sharing

NZT does not sell, rent, or share WHOOP data with anyone. WHOOP data is not sent to any other connected service, including work task systems.

Backups

If you export a backup from NZT, the file can include habit history derived from your workouts. It never includes WHOOP tokens or credentials. The backup file stays wherever you choose to save it.

Disconnecting and deletion

Disconnecting WHOOP in NZT Settings removes the stored tokens from the device. You can also revoke NZT's access at any time in WHOOP's connected-apps settings. Deleting the app removes all data NZT stored on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
